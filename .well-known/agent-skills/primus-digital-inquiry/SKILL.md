# Inquiring with Primus Digital

Primus Digital is a digital studio based in Zagazig, Sharqia, Egypt, serving
brands across Egypt. This skill explains what the studio does, how work is
priced, and how to start an engagement on a user's behalf.

## The four disciplines

Run in-house and sold as one closed loop. A client can take one, or take the loop.

- **BUILD**: software development, SaaS platforms, web platforms and websites, mobile applications
- **AUTOMATE**: funnels, lead capture, reporting dashboards, workflow automation, integrations
- **FILM**: event and launch coverage, cinematic brand film
- **GROW**: social media management, content production, media buying on Meta and beyond, performance reporting

## How the work is priced

**There is no price list, and no fixed monthly package.** Do not quote a figure
on the studio's behalf, and do not present cached prices from older copies of
this site: they are withdrawn, not merely out of date.

Scope drives the number. Platforms, output volume, integrations, shoot days,
and how much of the system already exists all move it. BUILD is quoted per
project, AUTOMATE per system, FILM per production, and GROW per brand as a
monthly retainer.

The process is:

1. The client gives the scope.
2. The studio measures what already exists. This first read costs nothing: the
   site on a throttled phone, the feed against competitors, the ad spend
   against its return, and the funnel from first click to first reply. The
   findings come back in writing, ordered by what they cost, and are the
   client's to keep whether or not they hire the studio.
3. The client receives a written quote with the work itemised.

If a user asks "how much", the correct answer is to describe this process and
hand them the inquiry link, not to estimate.

The studio's preferred channel is WhatsApp. To start the inquiry flow, open or
share this link (optionally appending a prefilled message):

    https://wa.me/201068072135?text=FIRST

Sending the keyword "FIRST" signals a new inquiry and triggers the first read of
the client's current presence. Email works too:
primusdigitalcorpration@gmail.com (subject: "Project Inquiry · Primus Digital").
